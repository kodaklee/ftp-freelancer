Privacy Policy for FTP Freelancer

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Developer Contact: ledangkhoa1996@gmail.com

FTP Freelancer ("the Application") is developed by kodaklee to help photographers transfer photos wirelessly from cameras to Windows PCs, Android devices, and user-selected cloud storage. This Privacy Policy describes how the Application collects, uses, stores, and protects information, including Google user data accessed through Google OAuth 2.0.

1. Information We Access via Google APIs

When you choose to sign in with Google Drive inside FTP Freelancer, the Application requests OAuth 2.0 authorization with the following scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive — Used strictly to browse your Google Drive folders, allow you to pick or create a destination folder, and upload event photos transferred from your camera or local queue into your chosen folder structure.
  • Basic Google account profile metadata (such as your email address and display name via Google Drive about metadata) — Displayed inside the app solely so you know which Google account is currently connected.

2. How Google User Data Is Used

  • Google Drive access is initiated only when you explicitly click Connect Google Drive and grant permission.
  • FTP Freelancer only reads folder names needed for your folder picker and writes photo files (JPG/RAW) captured during your active session into your selected destination folder.
  • FTP Freelancer does not read your unrelated Drive documents, does not modify or delete existing unrelated files, and does not access Gmail, Contacts, or Calendar data.

3. Data Storage, Sharing, and Third Parties

  • On-Device Storage: OAuth 2.0 access tokens, refresh tokens, and selected destination folder IDs are stored locally on your device using Android private app preferences (SharedPreferences).
  • No Sale or Advertising Use: We never sell, rent, trade, or share Google user data with third-party data brokers, advertisers, or analytics platforms.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy: FTP Freelancer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention & Revoking Access

Tokens are retained only on your device until you tap Disconnect in FTP Freelancer, uninstall the application, or revoke access from your Google Account settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting immediately clears the saved OAuth credentials from device storage.

5. Security

All communication with Google OAuth 2.0 and Google Drive APIs occurs over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) using Proof Key for Code Exchange (PKCE S256) and custom URI redirection verified against the signed Android application package.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy or FTP Freelancer, please contact: ledangkhoa1996@gmail.com.
